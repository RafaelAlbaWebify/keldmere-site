# Keldmere — prelaunch commercial workflow

**Status:** prepared procedure, not evidence of paying customers or accepted orders. Actual capacity, legal conditions and approved prices still need confirmation.

## 1. Receive enquiry
- Capture only necessary details: business, decision-maker/contact, existing site or new-site requirement, primary goal, target users/market and preferred follow-up.
- Classify: new website / redesign / monthly website care / managed website / Microsoft 365 (secondary).
- Do not solicit passwords, API keys, client records or privileged access by email.
- The website currently starts an email in the visitor's mail application; there is no CRM or automatic lead capture evidenced.

## 2. Qualify work and fit
**Website design/redesign:** number and type of pages, supplied content and branding, examples they like, domain/hosting ownership, integrations, forms/booking, accessibility needs, approval contact, proposed budget and intended date.

**Website care:** platform, hosting, administrator/owner, update responsibilities, plugins/dependencies, backup/restore status, existing incidents and whether an initial paid technical review/remediation is required. Do not promise coverage for an unsupported stack.

**Managed website:** required pages, hosting, domain control, asset ownership, monthly edit expectations, portability, exit terms and payment obligations.

**Microsoft 365:** tenant/users, desired review or change, licensing, authorised admin contact, least-privilege access and data-handling requirements.

## 3. Quote and contract
- Use `commercial/PROPOSAL-TEMPLATE.md`; clearly distinguish the service type.
- Confirm Webify Limited identity and required particulars; do not describe Keldmere as a registered business name without clearance.
- Specify the exact deliverables, pages, revision rounds, dependencies, exclusions, acceptance criteria, timeline, fees, taxes, third-party costs, change control and payment schedule.
- For recurring offers, define maintenance checklist, frequency, included edits, response hours, renewals, cancellation and handover. The current USD entry prices are **provisional**, not automatic entitlements.
- Obtain written acceptance and appropriate data/confidentiality agreements before access or changes.

## 4. Deliver and verify
- Work from an agreed scope; obtain approval for changes.
- Keep versions/source under controlled access; do not collect unnecessary production data.
- Test desktop/mobile, navigation, contact flow, performance, accessibility basics and any integration in scope.
- Deliver agreed handover, ownership/hosting credentials through approved secure channels and a record of outstanding limitations.

## 5. Invoice and close
- Invoice under the verified legal entity with appropriate tax treatment.
- Remove temporary access and record backup/hosting responsibilities.
- Record agreed support start/end, scope changes and any future review dates.
- Request permission before creating any identifiable case study. Do not invent proof of results.

## 6. Operating metrics (capture actuals; do not estimate as facts)
Log enquiry date, service, quote issued, quote accepted, actual work hours, third-party spend, rework/support hours and net receipt. Use actual observations to approve or revise price floors and tier allowances. **No current actual customer delivery dataset is documented in this repository.**
