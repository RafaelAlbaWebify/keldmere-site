# Minimal commercial operating procedure — prelaunch

## Enquiry
- Confirm purpose, business identity, contact preference and Microsoft 365 tenant scope.
- Do not request passwords, access tokens, sensitive user data or client records by email.
- Acknowledge personally; no promised response-time SLA until operating capacity established.

## Qualification
- In-scope service and demonstrable personal capability?
- Number of tenant(s), users, endpoints, licensing limitations, possible hybrid environment?
- Decision-maker/authorised IT contact available and access approvals possible?
- Confirm data protection, security, insurance and sector-specific needs before accepting work.

## Commercial documents
- Issue written scope and proposal based on commercial/PROPOSAL-TEMPLATE.md.
- Confirm entity: Webify Limited until an effective legal name change is approved.
- Agree fixed price, VAT treatment, payment terms, changes, delivery expectations, acceptance and exclusions.
- Obtain written approval and any confidentiality/data processing agreements required *before* access.

## Delivery
- Evidence minimisation, least privilege, no production edits without agreed authorisation.
- Maintain dated worklog, findings and any deviations from agreed scope.
- Validate deliverables, conduct handover, invoice correctly.

## Closure
- Remove temporary access; confirm customer retains administrative control.
- Agree deletion/retention of assessment evidence and customer communication.
- Document only permissioned, anonymised information for any future case study.
