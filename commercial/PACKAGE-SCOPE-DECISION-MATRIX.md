## Conservative lead triage
- **Starter-eligible candidate:** up to three simple pages, content and usable business identity supplied, no complex integrations or custom UX. Approval still requires hours/cost estimate.
- **Bespoke quote required:** premium art direction, content strategy, original branding, unusual layouts, third-party integrations, ecommerce, bookings, multilingual or advanced CMS.
- **Care assessment required:** platform, versions/plugins, responsible host, backups and access. Unknown condition or high-risk legacy implementation is not auto-eligible.
- **No implied SLA:** hosting performance, security operations, emergency response, unlimited edits or guaranteed outcomes require separate contractual commitments.

## Decisions to record before going live
1. Supported technology and hosting arrangements.
2. Revision round(s), change approvals and acceptance criteria.
3. Monthly checklist, backup/restore responsibility, response hours and billable overage rate.
4. Payment timing, subscription commitment and exit/portability process.
5. Measured build and support hours, direct costs, target contribution and professional tax/legal review.

**Do not conflate:** sample premium designs are not US$599 project deliverables or completed customer cases.
