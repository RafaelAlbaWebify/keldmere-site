# Microsoft 365 Health & Security Review — Scope v0.1

**Status:** commercial pilot proposal; not a binding public package. Price starts at €295 excluding VAT where applicable, conditional on scope confirmation. No claim of a completed Keldmere customer engagement.

## Intended customer
Small organisation already using Microsoft 365, looking for an independent, practical configuration and security hygiene review.

## Proposed baseline (validate before quoting)
- One Microsoft 365 tenant.
- Up to 15 user accounts, including administrator accounts.
- Remote, non-intrusive review of available configuration evidence.
- One 30-minute intake and one 30-minute findings discussion.
- One written prioritised summary in PDF form.
- Aim for delivery within five business days after all required access and information is supplied; **not a guaranteed SLA**.

The limits are **draft commercial assumptions** and must be agreed with the customer before any quote. Larger tenants, multiple domains, hybrid AD, complex compliance, Exchange migrations, remediation or incident response require separate estimation.

## Included checks
1. Tenant and licensing overview (inventory/observed plan suitability).
2. Users, privileged roles and available identity security indicators.
3. MFA and selected sign-in controls as visible in the tenant/approved evidence.
4. Selected security defaults/policies where licenses and access permit.
5. Device management posture if Intune or other agreed telemetry is accessible.
6. Prioritised quick wins, dependencies and limitations.

## Explicit exclusions
- Penetration testing, vulnerability scanning, SOC monitoring, forensic investigation or compliance certification.
- Full data protection assessment, GDPR legal advice, business continuity testing, backup restore testing.
- Remediation or changing settings during the assessment.
- Guarantee of security, breach prevention or achievement of compliance.
- Microsoft licence purchases/renewals, recurring support, end-user training.

## Access and handling
- Obtain written authorisation and scope from the customer first.
- Prefer read-only views or minimally privileged temporary access, agreed with the customer.
- No credentials, passwords or sensitive data sent by email.
- Avoid collecting personal content; record only necessary configuration evidence.
- Agree secure transfer, retention and deletion of notes and report beforehand.

## Acceptance criteria
- Named tenant and contact identified in approved statement of work.
- Agreed checks attempted and marked observed/not observed/not applicable.
- Findings rated critical/high/medium/low **as prioritisation, not CVSS scoring**.
- Report states evidence, recommendations, exclusions and unknowns.
- Customer receives the agreed summary and has a scheduled handover option.
