# Keldmere — brand direction and rules (draft)

**Commercial direction in the active PR:** founder-led web design, development, website care and proposed website subscription for small service businesses. Microsoft 365 work is secondary. The company operating the proposed brand is Webify Limited (Ireland); registration/clearance of the business name and disclosures are not yet confirmed.

## Design principles
- English-language, premium editorial presentation with typographic hierarchy, restrained colours, generous spacing and usable mobile layouts.
- Show work through three differentiated sample website designs: Hartwell & Co. (legal), BrightSmile (dental), Atrium (architecture). These are original AI-generated **samples**, not clients or commissioned work.
- The homepage currently uses a CSS cross-fade of three optimized WebP stills. There is **no production MP4 reel** in this branch.
- No invented testimonials, client results, offices, qualified professional credentials, endorsements or guarantees.
- Do not copy third-party agency images, source code, branding or layouts. Third-party sites can inform the standard of design, not be reproduced.
- Prioritise clear enquiry CTAs, readability, contrast, mobile cropping, and reduced-motion behaviour over ornamental effects.
